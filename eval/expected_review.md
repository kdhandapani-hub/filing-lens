## F02 (factual): How does Schwab describe the risk from clients moving cash out of bank sweep deposits into higher-yielding products?

**Candidate:** Schwab describes this as a liquidity risk: when short-term rates rise rapidly, clients move cash from sweep features into higher-yielding alternatives, and if outflows outpace cash on hand and portfolio paydowns—as in 2022 and 2023—Schwab may need higher-cost funding, which can be limited and subject to added regulatory requirements [3]. Such reallocation can lower interest-earning assets and/or require more higher-cost funding, constraining net interest revenue when rates rise rapidly [2]. Client cash allocation is sensitive to rate levels, with clients favoring purchased money market funds and certain fixed income products when yields exceed sweep rates [1].
