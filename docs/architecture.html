Filing Lens Architecture

Hybrid RAG over the latest 10-K filings of Raymond James, Schwab, LPL Financial and Stifel (Item 1A Risk Factors, Item 7 MD&A). Answers are cited or refused, traced end to end, and evaluated across chunking and retrieval configurations.

Design decisions

Two refusal gates

Grade refuses when nothing relevant is retrieved. Generate returns NOT_FOUND when retrieved text is relevant but doesn't answer, which caught the Morgan Stanley question at a 0.86 rerank score.

Namespaces as experiments

Each chunking strategy lives in its own namespace of one index, so every config runs against the same corpus and the comparison stays clean.

Per-firm retrieval

Comparison questions send each firm a focused query with a ticker filter and share a ~10-chunk budget, so a consolidated table isn't crowded out by segment tables.

Latency budget

Hidden reasoning disabled and clients warmed at start-up: 1.9–6.4s end to end in the demo. The remaining cost is the cross-region embedding call (eu-north1 → us-east-1).
